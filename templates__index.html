Method and limits

What this lab measures, and what it deliberately does not claim.

Three evidence channels

  • Server — HTML as served to a Googlebot user-agent, no JS.
  • DOM — text after JavaScript runs, which is what a rendered crawl sees.
  • OCR — screenshot read by Tesseract, which is what a vision reviewer sees.

A technique can win the first two and still lose the third. That is the finding, not an opinion.

What stays out of scope

  • No advertisements are served, and no account is ever submitted.
  • Clones are inert snapshots served with a sandbox policy.
  • The conversion module is a model, not a measurement of a live account.
  • Private and reserved addresses are refused, so the lab cannot be used as an SSRF pivot.
Hiding content from a review system is itself a policy violation regardless of whether the content is compliant. The lab exists to measure that trade-off, and its own numbers show the text-channel win does not survive vision review.
